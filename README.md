📌 Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the EasyTech Talent May 2026 data analysis challenge. As a Data Analyst engaged by a retail business, the goal was to analyze sales transactions and customer purchasing patterns to uncover revenue trends, evaluate discount strategies, and understand customer behavior — all visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance using key revenue metrics
Evaluate the impact of discounts on final sales and revenue
Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories
Understand customer purchasing behavior and payment preferences
Explore sales trends across different time periods
Build an interactive dashboard that communicates insights effectively
Provide actionable recommendations to improve sales and profitability

📁 Dataset
The dataset contains transactional retail sales data with the following fields:

User_ID
Unique customer identifier

Product_ID
Unique product identifier

Category
Product category (7 categories)

Price (Rs.)
Original price before discount

Discount (%)
Discount percentage applied

Final_Price (Rs.)
Price paid after discount

Payment_Method
Payment method used (5 methods)

Purchase_Date
Date of the transaction


Dataset size: 3,660 transactions | Time period: January – November 2024

🖥️ Dashboard Structure
The Power BI report is organized into 2 pages:

Sales Overview
KPI Cards: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Avg. Order Value, Total Discount
Revenue Trend by Month (dual-axis line chart: Revenue vs Discount)
Revenue by Category (sorted bar chart)
Payment Method Breakdown (donut chart)
Discount Analysis & Customer Behavior
Number of Orders by Category
Number of Orders by Month
Discount % vs Final Price (scatter plot with annotation)
Avg. Discount % by Category (bar chart with data labels)

Slicers available on both pages: Month | Category | Payment Method

💡 Key Findings
Total revenue of ₹757.28K was generated across 3,660 orders with an average order value of ₹206.91
Clothing is the top revenue category at ₹115K; Electronics is the lowest at ₹100K
₹175.29K was lost to discounts — approximately 23% of potential gross revenue
Revenue peaks in April–May and dips sharply in July–August before recovering in October
All 5 payment methods show a nearly equal split — Credit Card leads at 20.6%
Higher discount % directly correlates with lower final price collected per order

✅ Recommendations
Cap Discount Strategy (HIGH IMPACT)
Reduce discounts on top-selling categories (Clothing, Books, Home & Kitchen) which perform well regardless. Cap at 10–15% to recover ₹40K–₹60K in lost revenue.
Address the Mid-Year Revenue Dip (SEASONAL FIX)
Introduce flash sales, bundle offers, or loyalty campaigns during July–August to stabilize the seasonal dip.
Boost Electronics Category (GROWTH LEVER)
Electronics is the lowest revenue category despite high product value. Improve visibility, run category-specific promotions, or add warranty-backed offers.
Reward Top Payment Methods (RETENTION)
Introduce cashback rewards or exclusive discounts for Credit Card (20.6%) and UPI users to increase order frequency and average cart value.
